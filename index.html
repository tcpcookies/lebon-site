How We Work

From Concept to Container

A clear, four-step workflow keeps every project on schedule — from your first sketch to the loading dock.

01

Brief & Quote

Share your reference, target price and quantity. We respond with material options, sampling plan and FOB quote within 24 hours.

02

Sample & Sign Off

Custom sample produced in 7-10 days, shipped via DHL/FedEx. Free revision on materials, color and hardware until approval.

03

Production & QC

Bulk production in 25-40 days. Weekly progress photos + final AQL inspection report before shipment booking.

04

Logistics & After-Sales

We arrange FOB Shenzhen / HK / Ningbo shipments via your nominated forwarder or ours. 12-month product warranty included.

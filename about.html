About Us

Fifteen Years of Craft & Trust

Founded in Hong Kong in 2010, HK LEBON International bridges East and West for bag brands, importers and retailers who want reliable manufacturing without compromise on style.

Our Story

Built in Hong Kong. Designed for the World.

HK LEBON International Co., Limited was incorporated in November 2010 in the heart of Tsim Sha Tsui — Hong Kong's historic trade and luxury district. From our offices looking out across Victoria Harbour, we connect independent brands, established retailers and promotional buyers with carefully vetted bag manufacturers across mainland China.

What began as a single-line trading desk has grown into a full B2B sourcing partner — design support, material sourcing, sampling, QC and logistics under one roof. Every shipment leaves our partners' factories inspected, documented and ready for retail shelves in over 50 countries.

  • Hong Kong-registered trade entity (since 1 Nov 2010)
  • In-house design & sample workshop
  • Vetted manufacturing partners across Guangdong
  • Weekly progress photos for every bulk order
Philosophy

Bags Worth Carrying — Stories Worth Telling.

We believe a great bag is more than a product — it's a daily companion. That's why every HK LEBON style is built around three commitments: durable materials, honest craftsmanship and timeless silhouettes that don't chase micro-trends.

Whether we're translating a European designer's sketch for mass production or helping a startup develop its first hero SKU, the bar is the same: a product your customer will pick up, hold, and decide to keep.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ is 300 pieces per style and 100 pieces per color, with mixed styles across one shipment accepted. For custom development projects, MOQ may vary depending on materials — usually 500 pieces per SKU. Our sales team will confirm feasibility based on your brief.
Can you produce bags based on my design or reference photo?
Yes — that's our core service. Send us your reference photo, sketch or tech-pack and we'll prepare a material proposal, cost estimate and sampling plan within 24 hours. Sample production typically takes 7-10 days.
Do you provide samples before bulk production?
Yes. Pre-production samples are charged at sample fee + shipping, and the sample fee is fully refundable against your bulk order. We can ship via DHL, FedEx or your nominated courier in 3-5 days worldwide.
What is the standard lead time for bulk production?
Standard lead time is 25-40 days after sample approval and deposit receipt, depending on quantity and material sourcing. For urgent orders, we offer expedited production (15-25 days) with a 10% surcharge, subject to factory capacity.
What trade terms and payment methods do you accept?
Standard terms are FOB Shenzhen / Hong Kong / Ningbo, with CIF and DDP quotes available on request. Payment is T/T 30% deposit + 70% balance against B/L copy. L/C at sight and OA terms are available for repeat buyers and large accounts.
Do you handle product compliance and certifications?
Yes. We provide REACH, RoHS, CA Prop 65 and CPSIA compliance documentation. EN71 and other safety standard reports can be arranged where applicable. Test reports from SGS, Intertek and Bureau Veritas are available on request.
Can you do custom branding on the bag itself?
Absolutely. We support embroidery, screen printing, hot stamping, debossing, woven labels, metal plates and full-print sublimation depending on material. Our design team will recommend the best technique for your brand mark on first sample.
How do you ensure product quality?
Three QC stages: incoming material check (color, weight, defects), in-line inspection during production (every 2 hours), and AQL 2.5 final inspection before shipment. A detailed photo report is included for every order.
